Implementation notes

  • Concept A — Deployed. One image, minimal code. Best immediate impact once you have a strong action shot.
  • Concept B — Deployed. Works with as few as 4 images. The fade-right hint encourages scrolling and implies there's more to see.
  • Concept C — Deployed. Pairs a photo with a testimonial — strong trust signal at the point of decision on the pricing page.
  • Concept D — Replaces the existing pricing card design. High visual impact but requires one matched photo per package type.
  • Concept E — One hero + two thumbnails in a pure CSS grid. Punches above its weight visually, great for events.html or about.html.
  • Concept F — Photo as atmosphere, not information. The glassmorphism card floats above — works anywhere you need a booking nudge with emotional weight.
  • Concept G — 50/50 split to showcase gear quality and specs. Builds trust before the booking decision. Image can alternate sides per section.
  • Concept H — Social proof and photography combined into compact tiles. Each tile is one event type + one stat. Scales from 2 to 6 tiles.
