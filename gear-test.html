Obstacle types

📷Straight wall inflatable
Straight Wall Full-length cover for kneeling players. Creates a strong defensive line and natural team base boundary.
📷T-barrier inflatable
T-Barrier Angled cover allowing a player to protect two directions at once. The key mid-field obstacle for tactical play.
📷Cylinder inflatable
Cylinder 360-degree cover. No blind side — players must move tactically to get a clear shot. Great for solo defenders.
📷Corner piece inflatable
Corner Piece L-shaped cover ideal for protecting flanks and setting up ambush positions. Common near team start zones.
