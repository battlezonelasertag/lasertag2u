Headbands, not vests. Here's why.

Most laser tag hire operators use chest vests — heavy, slow to fit, and a genuine problem when you're cycling 50 kids through in 20-minute sessions. We made a different call when we started and haven't looked back.

Our sensor headbands weigh almost nothing, take seconds to put on, and fit every player regardless of age or size. No fumbling with harnesses, no frustrated kids, no slowdowns. At a busy school fete, that matters a lot.
