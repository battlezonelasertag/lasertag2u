How Laser Tag 2 U started

Laser Tag 2 U started in Port Stephens with one idea: bring commercial-quality laser tag to the events people were already running, instead of making them come to us.

Port Stephens and the wider Hunter region is full of the kinds of events that need a standout activity. School fetes. Community festivals. Sports days. Birthday parties. What didn't exist was someone who could show up with proper gear, build a real arena, run the whole thing professionally, and pack down at the end without leaving a trace.

That's what we set out to build. Since then we've delivered more than 500 events and put laser tag in front of tens of thousands of players across NSW. The model has never changed: you organise the event, we run the laser tag. Everything is handled.
